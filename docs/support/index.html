CrumbScope 支持

面包切片气孔（孔隙率）测量与对比

快速上手

  1. 拍摄或从图库选择一张照片——可以把多片切片排成整齐的网格一起拍；
  2. 选择网格尺寸（默认 4 × 6；只测一片时点 “Single Slice”）；
  3. 点 Analyze Grid；
  4. 结果里每一格是一种切片，点击任意一格可查看细节。

常见问题

两次测量的结果可以直接比较吗？
可以，但要固定拍摄条件：同样的距离和角度、切片在画面里占据的比例接近、照片长边不低于 1400 像素、并使用相同的网格设置。气孔越细的面包对分辨率越敏感，缩小照片会让结果偏低。

照片会被上传吗？
不会。App 完全离线，照片只在设备内存中处理，详见隐私政策。

需要什么设备？
iPhone，iOS 17 或更高版本。

结果看起来不准怎么办？
切片表面切得越平整、光照越均匀，结果越稳定。避免一侧阴影、反光，以及切片互相遮挡。测量的是切面的气孔面积占比，和面包的体积、比容不是同一个量。

CrumbScope Support

Porosity measurement and comparison for bread slices

Getting started

  1. Take or choose a photo — several slices can be arranged in a tidy grid and shot together;
  2. Pick the grid size (4 × 6 by default; use “Single Slice” for one slice);
  3. Tap Analyze Grid;
  4. Each cell is one slice. Tap a cell for detail.

FAQ

Can I compare results across photos?
Yes, if the capture is kept constant: same distance and angle, the slice filling a similar share of the frame, the photo at least 1400 px on the long side, and the same grid setting. Finer crumb is more sensitive to resolution — shrinking a photo biases the reading low.

Are my photos uploaded?
No. The app is fully offline; photos are processed in memory only. See the privacy policy.

What do I need?
An iPhone running iOS 17 or later.

The numbers look off.
A flatter cut and more even lighting make the measurement steadier. Avoid one-sided shadow, glare and overlapping slices. The app measures the pore area fraction of a cut face — not loaf volume or specific volume.
