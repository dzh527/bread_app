CrumbScope 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 12 日

CrumbScope 不收集任何数据。

  • 相机与照片：App 申请相机和照片图库权限，唯一用途是读取你选择的面包照片并进行分析。照片只在你设备的运行内存中处理，不会被上传、发送或保存到任何地方。
  • 网络：App 不包含任何联网功能，不向任何服务器发送请求，也不与第三方共享任何信息。
  • 账号：App 没有账号系统，无需注册或登录。
  • 分析结果：测量结果仅显示在屏幕上，App 不建立数据库、不写入文件、不保留历史记录，退出后即消失。
  • 第三方组件：App 不包含广告、统计分析或任何第三方 SDK。
  • 儿童：由于不收集任何数据，App 同样不收集儿童的个人信息。
  • 政策变更：如本政策发生变更，将更新本页面并修改生效日期。

CrumbScope Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026

CrumbScope collects no data.

  • Camera and photos: the camera and photo library permissions exist for one purpose — reading the bread photo you choose and analysing it. The photo is processed in your device's memory only. It is never uploaded, transmitted or stored anywhere.
  • Network: the app has no networking code. It makes no requests and shares nothing with anyone.
  • Accounts: there is no account system and nothing to sign in to.
  • Results: measurements appear on screen only. No database, no files, no history — closing the app discards them.
  • Third parties: no advertising, no analytics, no third-party SDKs.
  • Children: since no data is collected from anyone, none is collected from children.
  • Changes: any change to this policy will appear on this page with a new effective date.
